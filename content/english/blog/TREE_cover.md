### Matching Climate to biological scales

We had a perspective published in _Trends in Ecology and Evolution_ published this spring, which outlines how ecologists and evolutionary biologists should think about designing research questions and studies that better capture how organisms experience climate:  

[URL to paper](https://www.cell.com/trends/ecology-evolution/abstract/S0169-5347(25)00350-7) 


And a nice touch: we made it onto the cover of TREE's April 2026 issue! Featuring one of my favorite lizard species: _Phelsuma klemmeri_, the neon day gecko of Madagascar. Gorgeous lil' guys. 

The paper was covered by Yale's [Peabody Museum](https://peabody.yale.edu/news/a-new-framework) and [Institute for Biospheric Studies](https://yibs.yale.edu/news/new-framework-recent-paper-calls-biologists-rethink-how-they-analyze-impact-climate), and also covered by [Rutgers](https://deenr.rutgers.edu/news/dr-david-klinges-publishes-trends-ecology-and-evolution). Popular science outlets [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-paper-biologists-rethink-impact-climate.html) and [Meridia](https://www.meridia.news/news/paper-calls-for-biologists-to-rethink-how-they-analyze-the-impact-of-climate-2q67) picked it up too.
